Watermark proof of address documents for online verification

Proof of address files often include name, home address, account information and billing details. Before uploading them, a purpose-only watermark can help limit the copy’s context.

Common proof of address files

Utility bills, bank statements, tax letters, lease agreements and government letters are common address verification documents.

Watermark text examples

For ExampleApp address verification only; For bank account review only; For rental application only; For compliance check only.

Keep verification details readable

Address verification usually requires name, address, issuer and date. Use transparent text and avoid covering those fields.

Pair watermarking with redaction

If the recipient does not need account numbers or transaction details, consider redacting unnecessary information before or after watermarking.

FAQ

Should I watermark a bank statement used as proof of address?

If accepted by the recipient, yes. Keep required details readable and consider redacting unrelated transactions.

Is watermarking a replacement for redaction?

No. Watermarking adds purpose context; redaction removes information the recipient does not need.
