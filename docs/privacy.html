Privacy Policy for Watermark Assistant

Last updated: 2026-07-01

Watermark Assistant processes selected images locally in your browser. The extension does not upload your images, watermark text or settings to a developer server.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Personal information
  • Account information
  • Browsing history
  • Payment information
  • Remote analytics or usage tracking data

Local Data Processing

Images selected by you, watermark text, watermark settings and language preferences may be processed and stored locally in Chrome storage to provide editing, preview, saving and in-page insertion features.

Network Transmission

Watermark Assistant does not send your selected images, watermark text or extension settings to the developer's servers.

Permissions

  • storage: saves watermark settings, language preference and local image cache.
  • unlimitedStorage: supports local storage of multiple selected images.

Contact

For privacy questions or support requests, use the support channel listed on the Chrome Web Store item page.
